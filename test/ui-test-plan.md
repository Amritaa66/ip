# Amy UI test plan

## Test case 1: Exit immediately

**Aim:** Confirm that Amy prints the farewell and exits when the user enters `bye`.

**Inputs:**
```text
bye
```

**Expected output:**
```text
________________________________________________________________________________
  A     m   m  y   y
 A A    mm mm   y y
AAAAA   m m m    y
A   A   m   m    y
A   A   m   m    y
Hello! I'm Amy.
What can I do for you?
________________________________________________________________________________
________________________________________________________________________________
Bye. Hope to see you again soon!
________________________________________________________________________________
```
## Test case 2: Mark a task done

**Aim:** Confirm Amy marks an existing task as done and shows `[X]` in `list`.

**Inputs:**
```text
todo buy milk
mark 1
list
bye
```

**Expected output:**
```text
________________________________________________________________________________
  A     m   m  y   y
 A A    mm mm   y y
AAAAA   m m m    y
A   A   m   m    y
A   A   m   m    y
Hello! I'm Amy.
What can I do for you?
________________________________________________________________________________
________________________________________________________________________________
added: todo buy milk
________________________________________________________________________________
________________________________________________________________________________
Nice! I've marked this task as done:
  [X] todo buy milk
________________________________________________________________________________
________________________________________________________________________________
Here are the tasks in your list:
1.[X] todo buy milk
________________________________________________________________________________
________________________________________________________________________________
Bye. Hope to see you again soon!
________________________________________________________________________________
```

## Test case 3: Unmark a task

**Expected output:**
